# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus disagreement on ideas and evidence, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Report
abuse through GitHub's private reporting tools rather than in a public issue.
